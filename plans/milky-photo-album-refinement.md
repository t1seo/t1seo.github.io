# Milky album: shelf, cover and pages

## Direction

Refine the existing memorial album under the user's implementation authorization. The album must feel naturally inserted among several books on the right bookcase; its cover and open pages must match the painted room's quality. Preserve the exact accessible title `2011-2026 Milky`, the 26 real uncropped photographs, their native resolution and order, English controls, bounded lazy loading, keyboard navigation, still/reduced motion, existing audio and privacy. This is an art/UI refinement, not an album-engine rewrite.

Chosen design: muted sage/olive linen, a fine inset frame, small restrained foil dates above a larger elegant `Milky`, crafted spine and paper foreedge. Open pages use bright ivory archival paper, generous editorial margins, fine folios and subtle photographic print mounts. Use the existing local Cormorant face. No invented captions, dates, quotations, photo alterations, autoplay, ornament animation or new dependencies.
