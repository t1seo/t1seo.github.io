Beautiful depth, interesting asymmetric skyline and distant aerial infrastructure, very few subtle rain droplets on glass. Foreground: rich dark walnut floating desk runs across lower right two thirds, y 65%-84%, subtle cable details and tactile matte metal. Screen faces almost square toward camera, clean nearly rectangular screen surface, dark charcoal green screen blank except very faint cyan ambient light, no visible text or UI on the screen (a real live interface will be placed there). A slim mechanical keyboard aligned below it, discreet warm white backlights. Small matte black speakers beside monitor, ceramic coffee cup on right desk, tasteful small dark notebook. A warm amber mushroom desk lamp at x 82%, y 60% gives a small warm golden pool on the wood. Include a charcoal upholstered ergonomic office chair at far right lower corner, not obscuring the monitor. Left foreground near x 14% a dark low lounge chair and a small shelf of books as quiet silhouettes. A few recessed architectural light strips, dim under-desk cyan glow, an organic plant silhouette at far right edge.
Lighting: natural convincing reflected cyan city glow and local amber desk lamp only. Ink-black shadows retain material detail; refined filmic exposure. Emphasize cool exterior vs warm occupied room, soft haze and physical light falloff, subtle cinematic grain, real tactile materials, prestigious art direction. Dominant midnight petrol blue, charcoal, muted cyan, restrained antique amber; absolutely not a wall of oversaturated purple neon. The city is luminous, the room dim yet legible.
Constraints: one coherent highly crafted scene, no text anywhere, no logos, no watermarks, no website UI painted into art, no floating HUD, no game UI, no person, no extra monitors, no fantasy gadgets. Monitors/desk objects have correct perspective and believable real proportions. Make this beautiful enough to be a film still.
