# Walking motion attribution

The motion curves used with this artwork are adapted from:

Giovanna Catavitello, Yuri P. Ivanenko and Francesco Lacquaniti (2015).
**Planar Covariation of Hindlimb and Forelimb Elevation Angles during Terrestrial and Aquatic Locomotion of Dogs.** PLOS ONE 10(7): e0133936.

- Article: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0133936
- S1 Dataset: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0133936.s001
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- Copyright © 2015 Catavitello et al.

Changes: walking cycles aligned to 60% stance, endpoint detrending, root-height
centering and trunk-length normalization, toe-clearance extraction,
equal-subject averaging, resampling and rounding; retargeting to the
painted character with contact-constrained inverse kinematics and authored
secondary motion. The measurements are of six retrievers, not of Milky.
The authors do not endorse this adaptation. This attribution covers the derived
motion data; it does not change the separate artwork's provenance or rights.
