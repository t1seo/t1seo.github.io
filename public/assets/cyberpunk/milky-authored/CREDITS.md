# Authored canine clips

Skeleton and `Walk`, `Gallop`, and `Idle` animations by **Quaternius**, from the
[Ultimate Animated Animal Pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimateanimatedanimals.html).
Licensed under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
Original license text: [License.txt](License.txt).

The original Shiba Inu model's mesh and materials were removed. The retained
skeleton transforms, animation times, keyframe values, and interpolation modes
were preserved; only container indices and buffer layout were changed.
Source and reproducible extraction: `asset-sources/milky-authored/` in the
project repository.
