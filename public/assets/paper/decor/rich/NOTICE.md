## Processing

Only PNG-to-WebP format conversion was performed with `cwebp -near_lossless 80 -q 100 -m 6 -exact`. Original dimensions and every alpha-channel value were preserved. No cropping, resizing, recoloring, repainting, background removal or new image generation was performed. Near-lossless compression may adjust RGB values slightly. Source PNGs remain available at the URLs above; source and output SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `manifest.json`.

Downloaded and source declarations checked on 2026-09-29. The wording “Vintage” or “watercolor-style” describes the published work/style and does not establish a historical date or a hand-painted production process.
