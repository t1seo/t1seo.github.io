# Studio-matched props

These six props were generated for this project with the built-in `image_gen` tool, using the approved room as the style reference. They are **not public-domain stock illustrations**. Public landscape layers, ambientCG paper textures, licensed recordings and the original brand assets remain in use; their separate licenses still apply.

The earlier vintage illustrations and porcelain cat are archived and are no longer rendered in the active room. The replacement set follows one material direction: matte paper/felt, muted sage, warm cream, terracotta, gentle dimensional detail and diffuse upper-left light.

## Prompt set

Shared production prompt:

> Use case: style-transfer. The supplied room is the exact ART DIRECTION reference, not the object to include. Create a standalone transparent cutout asset for compositing into that room. Match the reference's high-end photographed handmade miniature look EXACTLY: matte tactile cellulose paper, finely flocked felt, warm natural material grain, convincing gentle volume, soft contact shadows, muted earthy colors and diffuse warm window light from upper left. Camera is front-facing with a modest downward view, as in the room. No watercolor painting, no vintage engraving, no outlines, no flat vector shapes, no glossy porcelain, no photoreal stock-photo cutout, no white sticker border. The complete subject fills 85% of the canvas and is isolated on genuine transparency; no floor, background, scene, labels or text. Ground-contact shadow can be faint translucent only.

Subject directions:

- **winter-tree.webp** — Small Nordic fir tree with muted olive and sage fibrous branches; restrained brass baubles, terracotta clay ornaments, tiny fairy lights, kraft star; natural flax basket and two small kraft gifts with cotton ribbons. Entire tree visible. No shiny red bows, snow or saturated ornament cluster.
- **sleeping-cat.webp** — Warm oatmeal/taupe sleeping cat curled in a natural oval, closed eyes, relaxed face, subtle cream markings, fine short felt fur. Viewed gently from above to rest on a rug. No porcelain, exaggerated features or plush seams.
- **autumn-pumpkins.webp** — Small cinnamon terracotta pumpkin, smaller cream pumpkin and dried oak leaf. Matte fibrous paper/clay like the room pots, subtle ribbing, natural short stems, no cartoon features.
- **spring-flowers.webp** — Squat speckled warm ivory vase with three pale cream cosmos and muted sage leaves. Papery petals, dusty ochre centers and the room's matte clay grain. No bright red/blue colors or glossy glaze.
- **winter-wreath.webp** — Thin organic loop of muted olive fir, small natural pinecones and one cream linen bow. Airy open center, physical miniature foliage, no bright red petals or shiny ribbons.
- **sparrow.webp** — Tiny perched sparrow in the exact room's matte miniature style. Oatmeal chest, taupe and olive-brown paper/felt feathers, natural anatomy and small eye/beak. Warm diffuse upper-left light, full feet visible, genuine transparency. No base, branch, text, aura, vintage illustration, watercolor or outlines.

All assets were inspected before integration, then converted as complete images to WebP with lossless alpha. Exact output dimensions and hashes are in `manifest.json`. The original base room was not replaced.
