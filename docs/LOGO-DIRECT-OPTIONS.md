## Validation

- Each final image visually inspected at source scale.
- Full-image `cwebp` resize/conversion only; no hand compositing, crop, recoloring, or alpha editing.
- True alpha channel present on all three, extrema 0–254. Corners are transparent; no baked checkerboard background.
- Final dimensions: 512 × 512 RGBA.
- Asset sizes: A 19,920 bytes; B 21,800 bytes; C 57,376 bytes.
- Full-image previews inspected at 16, 32 and 48 px.
- A retains the strongest facial anchors at 16 px. B's low horizontal silhouette needs more space; C's paper/fur details naturally simplify at 16 px. B/C are clearer at 32–48 px and above.
- Separate gallery provides light/dark comparisons. Browser-based final gallery verification is owned by the integration/gallery agents; this asset task does not claim browser QA.
- The user chooses which option to develop/apply. No candidate automatically selected.

## Final prompts

### A — 잉크 밀키

Create one ORIGINAL minimalist graphic logo, NOT an illustration or pet portrait. Subject: the face of Milky, a white Maltese with a broad black button nose, small relaxed eyes, short hanging ears and an asymmetrical forehead tuft. VISUAL LANGUAGE: extreme geometric economy, a late-1960s Japanese symbol / contemporary independent studio identity. Only FOUR warm ivory solid interlocking forms: one rounded triangular forehead/cheek face, two little ears, one soft muzzle. ONE small pointed forehead wisp only. Charcoal outer contour is thick and sculptural. Eyes are two small simple charcoal solid discs. Nose is one wide black rounded triangle. Mouth one tiny soft curve. Flat two colors only, absolutely ZERO gradients, ZERO fur strands, ZERO hatching, ZERO fluffy textured edges, ZERO layered hair slivers, ZERO shine, ZERO realistic modelling, ZERO little decorative internal strokes. Smooth silhouettes and spacious negative cuts only. Avoid round teddy-bear face: ears are short pendulous soft rectangles and muzzle broad. Calm appealing intelligent character. A clean iconic symbol an expert identity designer would choose for a personal site favicon. Warm ivory #f5efdf and near-black #262a27 ONLY. Genuine transparent background, square canvas. Single head centered with 14% clear margin on each side. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO BADGE, NO SHADOW, NO MOCKUP.

### B — 잠든 밀키

Create one ORIGINAL minimalist brand emblem: a WHITE MALTESE DOG curled up asleep, compact full body in a horizontal oval/comma pose, seen a little from above. The small head lies at lower right on one forepaw, short floppy ear folded beside cheek, wide little black nose, eyes closed as simple charcoal crescents. Curled fluffy tail at left wraps around rear. His silhouette should unmistakably read as a small sleeping white dog. Design language: elegant Scandinavian woodcut symbol, smooth broad organic interlocking areas, only SEVEN shapes total, no detailed fur. Ivory body, muted warm-sage shadow for curled tail/ear, nearblack small nose and eyelid, fine but decisive charcoal outer contour to work on white backgrounds. One single cream forehead wisp, 2 little breaks on tail contour at most. ABSOLUTELY NO HAIR STRANDS, no hatching, no repeated pointed tufts, no gradients, no nose texture, no realistic portrait, no glitter, no toy eyes. Reassuring and very quietly affectionate. Image should look like a timeless designer's vector logo when displayed at 24 to 40 pixels. Genuine transparent background, square canvas, one horizontal curled dog centered using 76% width and about 50% height. No moon/stars, no words, no letters, no badge, no background tile, no ground shadow, no mockup.

### C — 페이퍼 밀키

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE premium tactile layered-paper character icon of the actual white Maltese Milky in the two subject-reference photographs, designed as a personal website brand mark. Preserve Milky's rounded low forehead with swept wisps, short soft drooping ears, dark natural almond-round eyes, broad glossy-black nose and short little muzzle. Direction: an expressive three-quarter HEAD ONLY looking a little upward toward the right, not a frontal face, with just a subtle curious head tilt; visibly asymmetrical elegant silhouette. Physical warm-ivory handmade paper relief, three or four carefully stacked die-cut paper layers, gentle warm-grey contact shadows and restrained tiny paper grain, charcoal nose and eyes. The effect is sophisticated tactile paper marquetry, not furry photorealism, not clay, not plastic, not plush; no huge shiny toy eyes. Keep only two or three sculpted broad forehead tufts and restrained ear forms so the identity reads at 40px. Rounded cheek and ears belong to one compact head; no body, no neck frill, no Santa hat, no collar, no text, no letters. Isolated on a genuinely transparent square canvas, single mark centered occupying 76% of the canvas, equal generous transparent margins. No circular badge, no rectangle background, no mockup, no cast shadow outside the head.
