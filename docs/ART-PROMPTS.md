## Room
Use case: stylized-concept. Production web background artwork, one single full-bleed landscape image, 16:10 aspect ratio. Use the attached board's SOFT smooth illustrated room as style reference, but create ONE room, no board, no labels.
Cozy solo developer studio in warm softly rendered tactile 2.5D illustration, matte plaster and honey oak, gently rounded physical materials. Straight-on eye-level view. This is a compositing base layer; movable objects will be drawn live on top by a website.
Precise composition: left 38% of the image is an uninterrupted, calm warm cream plaster wall, very subtle grain, intended for a readable dark headline. A very large rectangular oak-framed window sits at x=43% to 80% of image, y=12% to 62%. Window aperture is a completely flat uniform muted pale blue, NO landscape, NO reflected objects, NO internal window dividers. This flat rectangle will receive a landscape layer in the actual website. Visible richly textured but restrained light oak frame all four sides and sill.
A continuous warmly textured oak desk spans the image from x=8% to 96%, surface at y=76% to 87%, thick rounded front edge at y=87% to 90%. Desk surface is EMPTY for live website interactive props. One partial warm wood desk chair at x=57% at the bottom edge, seen from behind with a folded cream knitted throw, subtly cropped by bottom. Leave area below desk mostly shadowed warm plaster and wood. Rightmost wall (x=83% to100%, y=0 to74%) is uninterrupted clear cream plaster reserved for a real hanging ID badge rendered live. No baked badge, no lanyard. No monitor, no lamp, no mug, no plant, no books, no vase, no curtains, no objects on desk. The scene must still feel architectural and welcoming with rounded warm oak material and handmade texture.
Lighting: soft even diffuse neutral daylight, very subdued natural contact shadows, NO hard dramatic sun beams, no strong baked colored light, no shadows of branches. The website will add moving sunlight and adjustable lamp light. Gentle depth at right wall corner if needed. Calm high quality storybook-adjacent soft 3D illustration, not a real estate photograph, not isometric, not pixel art, not vector flat, no text, no watermark. Beautiful material quality. Leave the room quiet and composed.

## Seasonal landscape atlas
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a single seamless-format 2 by 2 SEASON TEXTURE ATLAS for a website's window view. This is a production sprite sheet, not a labeled concept board.
Produce a square image divided into exactly four perfectly equal square quadrants. NO gutters, NO margins, NO labels, NO borders, NO typography. Each quadrant is a full independent landscape view with the SAME camera and precisely identical geography. We will use CSS to display only one exact quadrant at a time.
Scene: an exceptionally beautiful quiet alpine lake surrounded by natural mixed pine and deciduous forest, layered bluish mountains rising in the background, a small wooded peninsula projecting from the left into the lake and closer leafy branches framing the far upper left. The lake fills the bottom third, mountains the middle third, open softly colored blue sky upper third. Calm reflected light, atmospheric depth, no buildings, no boats, no people, no manmade objects. Style is sophisticated soft illustrated 2.5D environment art with tactile painterly details, matte natural colors, restrained quiet realism; not pixel art, not a photo, not flat vector, not excessive contrast. Pairs with a warm honey oak and cream cozy developer studio. All four scenes are diffuse DAYLIGHT with NO visible sun or moon: runtime lighting layers will create different day times.
TOP LEFT = SPRING: tender fresh green deciduous leaves, restrained pale pink blossoms on upper-left nearby branch, fresh green forest and pale blue lake, distant mountain peaks still snowy.
TOP RIGHT = SUMMER: exactly same geography, lush deep green foliage and full leafy branch, vibrant tranquil blue lake, distant blue mountain peaks, soft white small clouds.
BOTTOM LEFT = AUTUMN: exactly same geography, deciduous foliage golden amber, dusty rust and muted ochre mixed with evergreen pines, warm blue lake, upper-left branch with colorful leaves. Elegant warm colors, never fluorescent orange.
BOTTOM RIGHT = WINTER: exactly same geography, snow settles on all shores, pine boughs and mountains; deciduous trees are bare; nearby upper-left branch is bare with delicate snow; open lake water in center and thin frost at edges; pale cold blue sky. No falling flakes baked in, runtime particles will do that.
Align horizon, peninsula silhouette, mountain peaks and lake shoreline precisely across all quadrants. Four complete landscape paintings with perfectly straight central horizontal and vertical seams. Each frame occupies exactly 50 percent of width and 50 percent of height of the final image.
